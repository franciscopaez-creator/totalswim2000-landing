Getting started

How the Total Swim 2000 app works

Personalized training plans, video analysis and progress tracking — for individual swimmers and for coaches running their own groups. This is the Premium Plan guide; if you're looking for the cheaper, fully automatic option, see the Automatic Plan guide.

What is TotalSwim2000

TotalSwim2000 (app.totalswim2000.com) is the training platform behind Total Swim 2000, alongside the in-person classes.

It works for two kinds of accounts:

  • Individual swimmer — you get your own weekly plan and can upload videos for technical analysis.
  • Coach — you set up your own groups of swimmers (up to 4, depending on your plan), and each group gets its own plan and group video analysis.

What sets it apart from a generic app: every plan is reviewed and adjusted by Francisco Páez, the coach who founded Total Swim 2000 — not an algorithm working alone. The app builds a starting point from each swimmer's goal and level, but the final call is always human.

Getting started as an individual swimmer

  1. Go to app.totalswim2000.com from your phone or computer.
  2. Enter your email. You'll get a link in your inbox — no password needed.
  3. The first time you log in, the app asks a short questionnaire: your name, your goal (technique, distance, speed, competition, master, etc.), your level, the pool you train in, your available days and how much time you have per session.
  4. Based on that, the app builds your first plan and picks your tier (based on how many days a week you train).

From there you're registered: next time you go to the same address with the same email, the app recognizes you and takes you straight to your dashboard.

How the plan and video analysis work

Your plan is generated automatically based on your goal, level and available time, and it changes on its own as you move through the training cycle (for example, ramping up during higher-load weeks and easing off during recovery weeks). But Francisco reviews it and can adjust it by hand whenever needed — it's never left purely to the system.

Video analysis runs on cycles, based on your plan's frequency (say, once a month or every two weeks). When it's your turn, the Video section opens up for you to upload a clip; when it's not your turn yet, it shows the date it'll open again. Once uploaded, Francisco reviews it and the diagnosis appears under Diagnosis, with what you're doing well and what to fix — often pointing to specific Library exercises to work on it.

For coaches

If you coach your own team (whether inside or outside Total Swim 2000), you can use the app to build your groups' training plans.

How to sign up:

  1. Go to app.totalswim2000.com and choose the coach option on the first screen.
  2. Enter your name, the pool you train in, the equipment you use, and how long your class runs.
  3. Add your swimmers one by one: name, age, level, 100m freestyle time, and their individual goal (technique, distance, speed or competition).
  4. The app automatically groups your swimmers by that goal (up to 4 groups) and shows you the tier and price based on your days per week and number of groups.

How it works after that:

  • Plan — each group has its own workout for the day, but you see it all together: for each block (warm-up, main set, etc.) you can see what every group is doing, side by side.
  • Videos — video analysis is per group, not per swimmer: you upload a video of the group training and Francisco reviews and replies with that group's diagnosis.
  • Calendar, Library and Articles work the same as for an individual swimmer, just at the group level.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be an in-person Total Swim 2000 student to use the app?

No. The app works for any swimmer or coach, whether or not you train in person at PowerClub Panamá Pacífico.

Can I change my goal later?

Yes, message Francisco and your goal gets adjusted, updating your plan accordingly.

What if I miss my video upload window?

No problem — you can upload it whenever you're ready, you just won't be able to upload a new one until your plan's cadence has passed since your last one.

Do I need to install anything?

No, it's a website — it works from your phone's or computer's browser, nothing to download. You can "add to home screen" on your phone so it opens like an app.

How do I log in if I lost the email link?

Go back to app.totalswim2000.com and enter the same email — you'll get a new link.

Does it cost anything?

Yes, depending on the tier you choose (days per week, and for coaches also the number of groups). For current pricing, message us on WhatsApp.
