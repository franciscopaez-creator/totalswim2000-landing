Automatic Plan guide

How the Automatic Plan works

Your plan is generated and updated on its own every week, for a flat $30/month. If you'd rather have Francisco personally review your plan, check out the Premium Plan.

What the Automatic Plan is

This is the subscription version of TotalSwim2000 (entrena.totalswim2000.com): you subscribe once, pay $30 a month, and the app builds and updates your plan on its own every week — no waiting on anyone to review it.

Your plan is built with the same method Francisco Páez uses with his own team: it ramps up progressively, includes recovery weeks, and varies the exercises week to week. The difference from the Premium Plan is that here no one adjusts it by hand — it's 100% automatic, which is why it costs less.

Getting started

  1. Subscribe from totalswim2000.com or directly on this Hotmart link — $30/month, cancel anytime.
  2. As soon as your payment is approved, Hotmart shows you (and emails you) the access link to the app: entrena.totalswim2000.com.
  3. Go there with the same email you used to buy and request your access code. You'll get an email with a 6-digit code — enter it in the app (no password needed).
  4. The first time, the app asks a short questionnaire: your name, your goal (technique, distance, speed, competition, etc.), your level, the pool you train in, your available days and how much time you have per session.
  5. Based on that, the app builds your first plan right away.

Next time you log in, use the same email — the app recognizes you and takes you straight to your plan.

How video analysis works

It's not included in the subscription: it's a one-time $20 add-on, which you can buy anytime from the Video analysis section of the app. Once purchased, you fill out a form with your video (or a link to it) and get your technical feedback by email within 72 hours.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to pay before I can log in?

Yes — unlike the Premium Plan, this one activates as soon as your payment is approved on Hotmart.

How do I cancel?

From your Hotmart account, anytime. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

What happens if a charge fails (expired card, etc.)?

You get 5 grace days with normal access. If it's not resolved by then, access pauses until the next approved charge — you don't lose your progress, it just stays on hold.

Do I need to install anything?

No, it's a website — it works from your phone's or computer's browser. You can "add to home screen" on your phone so it opens like an app.

How do I log in if I lost my code?

Go back to entrena.totalswim2000.com with the same email and request a new code.

Can I switch to the Premium Plan later?

Yes, message us on WhatsApp or email and we'll help you switch.
